⚠ Security warning — leave this OFF unless you truly need it.
UPnP tells your router to open this Keep's ports to the public internet automatically, bypassing the firewall that normally shields every device on your network. That exposes the Keep directly to the internet, lets any program on your network open ports without your knowledge, and depends on UPnP — which has a long history of router security flaws. Manual port-forwarding is safer — you control exactly what gets opened. Only turn this on if you can't port-forward by hand and you accept the risk.
When on, opens your chat & voice ports so friends can connect without router setup. Already forwarded by hand (or using a tunnel)? Leave this off.
